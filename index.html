Sheet Layout Setup

Print one marked copy for yourself to build the answer key (fill it in and scan it in the Scanner tab), then print blank copies for students. Students bubble in their 7-digit Student ID on the generated form, so scanned sheets are matched to names automatically.

Printing tips: print at 100% / “Actual size” (no “fit to page”), on white paper, in black ink. The four black corner squares must stay fully inside the page — they are what the camera locks onto. One sheet holds up to 40 items (two columns of 20); for longer tests, split the exam into parts.

How this works

  1. Generator — choose items and choices, download the PDF, print one copy as your key and blank copies for students.
  2. Answer key — in Scanner, fill in the key sheet by hand, then either click the bubbles manually or scan the key sheet. Save it under a section name.
  3. Roster — in Students, paste ID, Name lines so scanned sheets become names instead of numbers.
  4. Grade — start live scanning and pass sheets under the camera, or upload photos. Each sheet beeps once it is saved.
  5. Analysis — see which items were hard, which wrong answer tempted the class, and print a class report.
Works offline. Everything — keys, roster, results — is stored in this browser on this device. Use Backup in the Students tab to move data to another device, and Restore to bring it back.

Privacy: no photos and no student data ever leave the device. The engine runs entirely in this browser.
